recorded data today.** Every timestamped AI decision in this repository was
generated by the ALPHA_RND and C12_PROSPECTIVE tracks, both of which began
recording in late September 2026. There is no earlier decision stream.

## What a broader test would require

To span regimes, decision artifacts must exist for other dates. Options:

1. **Wait for forward data** (clean): each new week adds ~10 intents; ~3
   months yields a second regime window. Zero cost, zero contamination.
2. **Retrospective inference** (contaminated): run TradingAgentsGraph on
   PIT-sliced historical dates pre-October. Even with perfect input
   filtering, the model plausibly knows the outcomes — results are
   hypothesis generation, never verified alpha. Cost: each ticker-session
   ≈ one full debate pipeline (~$1-3 est. by duration_s≈380s); a minimal
   useful grid ≈ 10 tickers × 8 sessions ≈ 80 runs ≈ $80-240. Not run;
   flagged as the smallest useful contaminated experiment if desired.
3. **Non-LLM baselines** (clean): mechanical signal-free strategies are
   possible but answer a different question — excluded.

## Verdict

- A broader defensible historical backtest is **not possible today**: one
  recorded month, one regime. No holdout exists and none was manufactured.
- Within recorded data, evidence consistently points one direction: the
  LONG pipeline loses money across two independent tracks (ALPHA -2.40%,
  C12 -1.55% NAV), while hypothetical SHORT analysis is mildly positive but
  non-executable.
- The gap filter helps only against pre-entry event gaps; C12 shows the
  remaining dominant failure mode is post-entry drawdowns with no
  mechanical profit/loss discipline — profit protection (Exp D, +313) and
  tighter risk geometry address that leg, not C.
- Missing for validation: a second regime's recorded decisions; intraday
  fill data; short-borrow feasibility.
